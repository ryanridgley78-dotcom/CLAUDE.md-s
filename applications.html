Ministry Focus

What We Are Restoring

We are committed to restoring the original design of YHWH by raising up men to become the strength of the house; the righteous foundation, the established tent pole by which the household stands.

The Work Begins With the Man

This work does not end with the man. It begins there.

A man built, refined, and established in righteousness is called beyond personal strength into restorative function. He is to embody pure and undefiled fear of YHWH before Elohim our Father: to redeem the desolate, to gather widows and orphans, and to bring them under the covering and order of a righteous, functioning household in the Kingdom of Yeshua.

The Calling

In this, he remains set apart; unstained by a world that abandons, neglects, and rejects covenant responsibility.

This is the work.
This is the calling.
This is the intent of James 1:27.

The Original Design

It is a trustworthy declaration: if a man aspires to this office; to act as a redeemer, to establish order, to extend covering, and to build his household as a refuge for the desolate; he desires a noble work of YHWH, as written in 1 Timothy 3:1.

Therefore, we do not merely build men; we establish households of refuge: ordered, covenantal structures where the desolate are restored, the abandoned are gathered, and the purpose of YHWH is made visible in the earth.

This is the original design.
