Donate

Your support helps YHWH's Misfits continue building a virtual qahal for Torah study, fellowship, coaching, leadership development, and practical ministry service.

Donations will be used for the Widows/Orphans program, including application review, counsel, assessments, coursework, care planning, and practical program needs.

Ministry Support Priorities

Contributions help strengthen the systems, teaching resources, and elder-led program work needed to serve applicants and members with clarity, accountability, and care.

  • Widows/Orphans program support, review, and care planning
  • Teaching resources, coursework, and assessments
  • Application review, member support, and online gathering tools
  • Future gatherings and practical ministry development

Stewardship

Donations should be given freely, with discernment, and without pressure. YHWH's Misfits desires to steward support toward work that equips households, strengthens the assembly, and serves those seeking truth.

Donation Access

Online donation processing is being prepared. Until a donation link is connected, contact the ministry directly for giving questions or support opportunities.
