What We Are Building

We are not building an audience. We are establishing a virtual qahal for testing, correction, household order, and covenant readiness.

We are a people examined by Scripture and prepared to walk in order.

Torah as Practice

Scripture tested, studied, and walked out as living obedience instead of traditions of men.

Households in Order

Men strengthened, widows and orphans covered, and families restored under righteous order.

Remnant Readiness

Fellowship, discernment, correction, and preparation for the days ahead.

Our Purpose

YHWH's Misfits exists as a virtual qahal for those called to the remnant, where all things are tested through Scripture and weighed with discernment.

We gather to study Torah, prophecy, the canon, and extra-biblical witnesses including 1 Enoch, Jubilees, and 2 Esdras; seeking truth over tradition.

What We Establish

Our mission is to establish Torah as living practice, strengthen biblical household order, restore patriarchal responsibility, and prepare families to remain set apart.

We hold that households require clarity, structure, accountability, and readiness for the days ahead, rooted in obedience to YHWH and faith in Yahushua.

How We Equip

Through Torah study, fellowship, coaching, leadership, and practical application, YHWH's Misfits equips those who desire to walk in covenant truth.

We are building a place for testing, correction, sharpening, and faithful community; among those willing to be examined by Scripture.
